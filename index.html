Concord Switch Nigeria Limited (concordswitch.com) is an African fintech ecosystem offering modern payment routing, business wallets, smart POS solutions, merchant accounting, bill payments, and financial software—built to make commerce seamless. (Note: Commercial internet connectivity and fiber infrastructure in our corporate family are managed by our sister entity, Youfit Connect, also part of 4th Group of Companies).

From powering fast POS settlement for regional businesses to developing scalable microservices, consumer virtual accounts, and financial dashboards, our Awka hub serves as an epicenter of fintech talent incubation, software engineering, and youthful capacity building.
